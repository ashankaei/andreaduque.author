2. What information do I collect?

When you subscribe to my newsletter, I collect your name and email address.

Mailchimp may also record technical information, such as your subscription date, time, and IP address, to document your consent.

With your consent, I use your information to send occasional emails about Detours, my writing journey, sneak peeks, and future book news.

The legal basis for sending these emails is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR.

4. How is your information stored?

I use Mailchimp (Intuit) to manage my mailing list and send newsletters.

Mailchimp processes subscriber information on my behalf, including in the United States, subject to applicable data protection safeguards.

For more information, please see Mailchimp's Privacy Statement.

5. How long do I keep your information?

I keep your information on my active mailing list until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent.

Limited records may be retained where necessary to demonstrate compliance with legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, as well as restriction of processing, data portability, and objection where applicable.

You may withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, please contact me at info@andreaduqueauthor.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority in Germany.

7. Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in every newsletter email.

8. Updates

I may update this policy if my data processing practices change.
