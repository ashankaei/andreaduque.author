"I spent years thinking my stories would stay in notebooks nobody saw."

I grew up with a book in my hands — Harry Potter first and hardest, then everything after it. I trained as an accountant, built a career on numbers, and told myself the writing could wait.

Then I became a mother. Somewhere between the sleepless nights and the small, stolen hours after bedtime, I opened my imagination back up. Detours, my debut novel, is what came out of it: the book I always meant to write, finished in the margins of a very full life.

These days I'm a mom to a toddler, a reader of anything with a well-worn spine, and — as of this edit — officially a working novelist.

Detours

currently in edit

Emma Sullivan believes that doing everything right will keep her safe — until Bennett Group turns her down for the job she's spent years chasing. On impulse, she books a solo trip across Europe, every hour mapped out in advance. Then the stranger she's spent almost a year running past at dawn turns out to be Julian Bennett, the president's son, and one unplanned detour to Rome unravels every plan she's ever made.

A strangers-to-something romance about what happens when the version of your life you refuse to choose finally catches up with you.
